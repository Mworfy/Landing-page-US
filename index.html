We're excited to announce a new partnership between AIESEC US and Educations.com — a global platform connecting students with world-class educational opportunities.

Don't just go abroad. Come back with credits.

What if your international experience could count toward your degree?

Through our new partnership with the Centre for Global Citizenship Studies (CGCS), everyone who goes on exchange with AIESEC now has the opportunity to combine a real-world international experience with accredited, transferable US university credits — at a special partner price.

You don't have to choose between living a meaningful experience abroad and building your academic profile. With AIESEC and CGCS, you do both at the same time.

About Us

AIESEC is a global platform for young people to develop their leadership potential through practical experiences of many kinds, including internships, volunteering opportunities, and more.Founded in 1948, AIESEC is a non-governmental and not-for-profit organization entirely run by youth for youth.
