Support the Next Generation of AIESEC United States Leaders

Join our mission for equal access to leadership opportunities for our membership

Through the AIESEC US Legacy Fund, your donation provides scholarships that cover flights and conference fees, ensuring students from all backgrounds can attend national leadership conferences and grow as global leaders.

About Your Donation

The AIESEC US Legacy Fund ensures that no student leader is left behind due to financial barriers. Our national conferences are where members build skills, connect across cultures, and prepare to lead in their communities.

Your gift directly supports:

  • Flights and travel support so members can attend national conferences.

  • Conference fees that unlock access to leadership development spaces.

  • Equal opportunities for our students to grow as global leaders.

By giving monthly, you provide ongoing support that sustains AIESEC’s mission and guarantees more students can step into leadership opportunities year after year.

Double Your Donation Today! Ask your employer if they can match your contribution and amplify the difference you make.

About AIESEC

What is AIESEC?

AIESEC, the largest youth-led organization in the world, is a global platform for young people to develop their leadership potential through practical experiences of many kinds, including internships, volunteering opportunities contributing to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and more. Founded in 1948, AIESEC is a non-governmental and not-for-profit organization entirely run by youth for youth.

We create value-driven young leaders enabling them to develop the values we believe leaders should live by.

Is AIESEC a nonprofit?

Yes, AIESEC US Inc. is a 501 (c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization. Contributions to this organization are tax-deductible to the full extent of the IRS laws. We are able to accept donations to further our goals of providing leadership development experiences to students and young people through cross-cultural exchanges and leadership conferences, as well as general organizational support.

Is AIESEC affiliated with a political party?

No. AIESEC is a nonpartisan organization.

Who supports AIESEC?

We’re supported by generous individual donors and companies.

General Donation Questions

Why should I donate to AIESEC? What do my donations support?

AIESEC is an organization that aims to develop leadership skills in young people and create a positive impact in communities around the United States. By donating to AIESEC, you are supporting our mission and helping us create a better future for all.

Here are some specific reasons why you should consider donating to AIESEC:

  1. Developing future leaders: AIESEC offers leadership development programs that empower young people to become effective leaders who can make a positive difference in the world. Your donation will help us reach more young people and provide them with the tools and resources they need to succeed.
  2. Creating positive impact: AIESEC's programs focus on important global issues such as sustainability and cultural understanding. By donating to AIESEC, you are supporting initiatives that have a tangible impact on local communities and contribute to creating a better world.
  3. Supporting diversity and inclusion: AIESEC is committed to promoting diversity and inclusion in all its programs and activities. Your donation will help us reach more young people from different backgrounds and underrepresented groups to ensure that everyone has an equal opportunity to develop their leadership skills and make a difference.
  4. Investing in the future: By supporting AIESEC, you are investing in the future of young people and the world. Your donation will help us continue to grow and expand our programs, reach more communities, and create a positive impact for generations to come.

Overall, your donation to AIESEC will support our mission of developing young leaders who can create positive change in the world. We appreciate any support you can give and thank you for considering AIESEC as a worthy recipient of your donation.

Do you publish your financial statements or an annual report?

Yes. As a nonprofit organization, AIESEC is required to disclose all financial information, which you can find here.

How can I financially support AIESEC?

AIESEC accepts gifts, grants, IRA charitable rollovers, in-kind donations, and more. Monetary donations can be made online or via a check sent to 85 Broad St, 17th floor, New York, NY, 10004. If you have any questions about how to give or get involved, please email us at donate@aiesecus.org or call us at (212) 757-3774, and we would be happy to help.

Is my gift tax deductible?

Yes, all donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent of U.S. law.

Can my company match my donation?

Possibly! Many companies offer matching gift programs. Find out if yours does by reaching out to them.

Where can I find my donation receipt?

If you make a gift using our online portal, your receipt will be sent to the email address you provide during checkout. If you did not make an online donation and need a donation receipt, we will send your receipt to the address provided or listed on your check. If you can’t find your receipt, we can resend it. Please contact donate@aiesecus.org or call us at (212) 757-3774, and we would be happy to help.

Can I make a recurring donation?

Yes! Just select monthly or annual when you go to make your online donation.

Is my online donation secure?

Yes. We partner with Donorbox to ensure all online donations are secure. Donorbox is PCI-compliant and secured by SSL/TLS encryption.

I don't want to give online. Where can I mail my donation check?

You can mail your gift to our office at 85 Broad St, 17th floor, New York, NY, 10004.

Can I make my gift through a wire transfer?

Yes. Please use the following information to complete a wire transfer gift:

  • Bank Name: JPMORGAN CHASE BANK, N.A
  • Branch Account Number: 371240516
  • Bank routing #: 021-000-021

I can't make a monetary donation right now. Are there other ways I can support AIESEC?

Absolutely! You can still support us by spreading the word about our mission to your friends, family and network. To stay up to date on all AIESEC happenings, join our mailing list.

Corporate Partners

My company is interested in supporting AIESEC. Who do I contact to find out more information?

AIESEC accepts grants, checks, in-kind, and employee-matching gifts from corporations that are aligned with our mission and values. Please call (212) 757-3774 or email donate@aiesecus.org with your company name and contact information, and we will reach out to get started.

My company is interested in sponsoring an AIESEC event. Who do I contact to find out more information?

AIESEC welcomes corporate sponsorships of our events which happen four times a year. For sponsorship requests, please email donate@aiesecus.org or call (212) 757-3774 and we will be in touch to get started.

My company is interested in sponsoring a young person to go on a Global Volunteer exchange. Who do I contact to find out more information?

AIESEC welcomes corporate sponsorships for young people to go on a six-week Global Volunteer exchange and make a positive impact abroad. For sponsorship requests, please email donate@aiesecus.org or call (212) 757-3774 and we will be in touch to get started.
