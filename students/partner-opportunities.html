Don't just go abroad. Come back with credits.

What if your international experience could count toward your degree?

Through our new partnership with the Centre for Global Citizenship Studies (CGCS), everyone who goes on exchange with AIESEC now has the opportunity to combine a real-world international experience with accredited, transferable US university credits — at a special partner price.

You don't have to choose between living a meaningful experience abroad and building your academic profile. With AIESEC and CGCS, you do both at the same time.

How it works:

It's simple. You sign up for an AIESEC exchange — whether that's a volunteer project, a professional internship, or a leadership initiative anywhere in the world. While you're living that experience, you enroll in a CGCS online course that complements what you're doing on the ground. The course is fully asynchronous, meaning you study on your own schedule, from wherever you are in the world. When you complete it, you walk away with 3 official US university credits on a transcript issued by Hartwick College (New York, USA) — recognized by universities and employers worldwide.

Picture this: you're volunteering with a community health project in Southeast Asia through AIESEC. At the same time, you're enrolled in CGCS's Global Health Perspectives course — turning your daily on-the-ground experience into structured academic learning, a reflection portfolio, and 3 transferable university credits. Two things happening at once. Double the value.

A special price for AIESEC exchange participants:

As part of this partnership, everyone who goes on exchange with AIESEC US gets access to CGCS courses at an exclusive discounted rate. International experience and academic credit — together, for less.
