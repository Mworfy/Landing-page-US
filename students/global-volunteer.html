Earn College Credit with Your AIESEC Experience!

Thanks to our recent partnership with accredited educational institutions, students who join an AIESEC volunteering or internship program abroad now have the opportunity to earn up to 9 college credits while living a life-changing international experience.

Credit-Bearing Option

Earn up to 9 transferable college credits through our academic partners. Make your impact abroad count toward your degree.

Non-Credit Option

Complete the program and receive a certificate of completion, gaining valuable skills and international exposure.
