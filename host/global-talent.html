Youth Global Employability Report

The Youth Global Employability Report contains the results of a survey by AIESEC. The aim is to understand the global job market for students and graduates, especially with regard to the global mobility of young talent. To this end, over 400 companies from 65 countries were surveyed. The report contains the results in terms of the expected skills employers expect when hiring young talent.

The world in your office

Your employees will interact, coach, and lead the group of interns, fostering creativity and a fresh perspective, driving innovation and cross-cultural understanding.

Here’s how it works:

Lets talk about what's possible:

Let’s have a  meeting to talk about the project, which profiles are you looking for, job description and check your company’s eligibility to host J1 visa interns.

Fill in Job  Description and Trainnig Plan:

Select the skills and the define the profile you’re looking for. After this is done, the opportunity will be live in our global platform, aiesec.org.

Screening & Interview:

We’ll do the screening for you and present you with a shortlist of profiles for you to interview.

Internship Delivery:

Once you’ve selected your intern(s), we’ll take care of the rest, all the way up until the intern finishes their experience.
